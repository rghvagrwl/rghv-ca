## Alan Fletcher — The Art of Looking Sideways

The Phaidon hardcover (2001), ISBN 9780714834498, is modeled at 210 × 250 × 60 mm. The People’s Graphic Design Archive records its 1,064 pages and all three dimensions. The Canadian Centre for Architecture catalogs the same edition and notes its unusual pagination by spreads. The printed white case carries a continuous sideways text composition across the back, spine, and front, with a red Phaidon band along the foot. [Physical dimensions and cover photographs](https://peoplesgdarchive.org/item/17766/the-art-of-looking-sideways) · [Canadian Centre for Architecture edition record](https://www.cca.qc.ca/en/search/details/library/publication/46984749)

The front texture is rectified from The Print Arkive’s photograph reproduced in the graphic design archive. The spine preserves the original four lines of black and red type isolated from picture 2 of St Philip’s Books’ first-edition copy. The small author credit, solid red foot band and contrasting-stroke Phaidon mark are reconstructed on clean off-white stock. The reverse is rectified from picture 3. The model approximates the binding sheen. ## Ellen Lupton — Thinking with Type

The third, revised and expanded edition (2024), ISBN 9781797226828, is a 256-page paperback. Chronicle lists a 7 × 8½ in trim, modeled as 178 × 216 mm. The approximate 25 mm spine follows the American Institute of Architects’ design shop’s measurement of the finished book. [Publisher’s edition record](https://www.chroniclebooks.com/products/thinking-with-type-1) · [American Institute of Architects’ dimensions](https://designshop.aia.org/products/thinking-with-type)

The front and back use rectified photographs from the Minnesota Center for Book Arts. They preserve the blue panels, printed specimen borders, and original lettering. The spine is reconstructed from Draw Down’s photograph on a flat blue ground. Newly set condensed red credits and black serif display type, fine dividers, and a redrawn Princeton Architectural Press mark follow the printed sizes and positions. Typeface matches, paper tint and finish are visually estimated. ## Josef Albers — Interaction of Color

Yale University Press’s 50th Anniversary Edition (2013), ISBN 9780300179354, is a 208-page paperback. Yale’s UK record gives a 152 × 234 mm trim. The modeled 15 mm thickness follows a bookseller’s full three-axis measurements for the same ISBN. [Publisher’s edition record](https://yalebooks.co.uk/book/9780300179354/interaction-of-color/) · [Physical dimensions](https://www.bookdelivery.com/ie-en/book-interaction-of-color-50th-anniversary-edition/9780300179354/p/18948426)

The warm-colored front is Yale’s flat artwork. The cool-colored reverse uses the matching flat retail image, cropped to remove its presentation frame. The spine’s author, title and anniversary credit are newly typeset to match the contrasting faces and measured positions in Yale’s artwork and Outset Galway’s photograph. Its fine oval imprint retains the photographed monogram, with a reconstructed outline. Typefaces, paper tint and finish are visually estimated.

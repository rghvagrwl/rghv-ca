# Art, architecture, photography and design editions

Dimensions are width × height × depth in millimetres, rounded from the publisher or distributor’s physical record. Each front uses that ISBN’s publisher-supplied TIFF converted to sRGB. The Art Book also has the original back jacket. Other back covers and all spines are reconstructions using matching cover colors, compatible typography, and compact original back-cover descriptions. These panels approximate the printed editions. The original cover art belongs to the respective artists and publishers.

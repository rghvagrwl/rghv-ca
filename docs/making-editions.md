# Making and creative practice editions

Dimensions below describe closed copies in millimetres, in width × height × thickness order.

## Atomic Habits

James Clear’s 2018 US Avery hardcover has 320 pages. Penguin Random House lists ISBN 9780735211292 at 161 × 235 × 27 mm. The cream jacket carries a dotted metallic title and a black author credit. [Publisher’s physical specification](https://prhinternationalsales.com/book/?isbn=9780735211292)

The front uses Penguin Random House’s flat artwork. A photograph of the Avery hardcover establishes the placement and proportions of its spine printing. The dotted title, four-line subtitle, and stacked author name use their isolated original artwork from the Penguin Audio/Playaway print insert. The Avery imprint uses the registered mark. The warm gold ink color is an approximation of the foil. The back was rectified from a photograph showing the matching ISBN. ## Steal Like an Artist

Austin Kleon’s 2012 Workman trade paperback has 160 pages, ISBN 9780761169253. Hachette gives a 6 × 5.9 inch trim. The 14.3 mm thickness follows the 0.5625 inch measurement in its trade distribution record. The near-square, black wrapper has white hand lettering and red spot color. [Publisher’s edition](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/austin-kleon/steal-like-an-artist/9780761169253/) · [Publisher’s dimensions catalog](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/F24_HBG_Gift_Catalog_FIN-Adult-WEB.pdf) · [Trade dimensions](https://bulkbookstore.com/steal-like-an-artist-10-things-nobody-told-you-about-being-creative-9780761169253-1)

The front and back are Hachette’s flat plates. The spine isolates the white hand lettering and outlined Workman mark from its product rendering and places them on flat black stock. Cover design by Austin Kleon. ## Creative Selection

The US Picador trade paperback by Ken Kocienda, ISBN 9781250203410, was published in December 2019 and has 304 pages. Macmillan’s trade record gives 5.4 × 8.25 × 0.85 inches, modeled at 137.2 × 209.6 × 21.6 mm. Alex Merto designed its wrapper, which carries six bands of color. [Publisher’s edition](https://academic.macmillan.com/academictrade/9781250203410/creativeselection/) · [Macmillan trade dimensions](https://bulkbookstore.com/creative-selection-inside-apples-design-process-during-the-golden-age-of-steve-jobs-9781250203410)

The front is Picador’s original artwork. The back comes from a perspective-corrected photograph of that printing, including its designer credit and ISBN. The spine is an interpretation: it continues the wrapper’s six colors, with the actual title, author lettering, and Picador wordmark isolated from the front. Their spacing and placement on the spine are estimated.

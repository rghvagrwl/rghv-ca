## Physical model

The trim dimensions are estimates for each publication format. The model uses 6⅝ × 10¼ inches for the later issues and a slightly wider 6⅞-inch trim for the 1966 issue. The exact measurements and paper caliper of each photographed copy are unknown. [Printer’s format guide](https://printninja.com/design-a-modern-comic-book/)

The original six use two staples through the folded spine, with printed covers around a thin paper block. The Star Wars series record identifies a glossy cover and newsprint interior. In the initial six, five issues are modeled at 36 pages. Amazing Spider-Man #300 has 52 pages and a correspondingly thicker folded edge. [Star Wars series record](https://www.comics.org/series/2406/) · [Spider-Man #300 publication record](https://www.comics.org/issue/44451/)

A separate clear film encloses each comic. The sleeve width follows the current or regular/silver bag size and the flap folds over the reverse. The material reference is clear 2 mil polypropylene. [BCW sleeve specification](https://www.bcwsupplies.com/silver-comic-book-bags)
